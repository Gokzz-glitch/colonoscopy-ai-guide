Research Discovery • Integrity Audit

The ChakraModel Pivot: From Model Building to Forensic Science

The ChakraModel project began with an ambitious goal: engineering a lightweight vision transformer capable of matching clinical-grade polyp detection at ultra-low inference latency.

However, rigorous empirical validation uncovered a startling reality. When our 309-million parameter ChakraTransformer (ViT-L/16) was evaluated across standard datasets, it scored a disappointing 0.771 Dice on CVC-ClinicDB—trailing behind a 2015 U-Net baseline.

Investigating this discrepancy led to our most consequential discovery: the academic benchmark itself was fundamentally compromised. Standard evaluation splits were leaking consecutive video frames between training and test sets, inflating reported scores by 12% to 15% across dozens of published medical AI papers.

Forensic Audit Highlights

1. CVC-ClinicDB Video Leakage: All 62 test frames in the canonical PraNet split originate from the identical video sequences as training frames (30 near-duplicate links).
2. ColonDB & CVC-300 Overlap: Share 58 exact-pixel identical images—pooling them constitutes scientific double-counting.
3. Negative Frame Blindness: Standard benchmarks test models only on frames with polyps, completely ignoring alarm fatigue caused by false positives on healthy tissue.

The Ground Truth Gap

Bridging Two Disconnected Worlds

Medical AI literature and clinical practice often speak entirely different languages. Here is how key realities contrast across domains.

Domain Dimension Academic Machine Learning Conception Clinical Gastroenterology Reality The ChakraModel Resolution
Primary Success Metric Pixel-wise Dice Similarity (>0.92) and IoU on tightly cropped polyp images. Adenoma Detection Rate (ADR), reducing interval cancer deaths (Corley 2014), and minimizing complications. Evaluate models on per-polyp detection latency and false-alarm frequency per minute of withdrawal.
Dataset Composition 100% positive frames containing prominent polyps under optimal lighting. >95% healthy mucosa, residual stool, bile pools, active bleeding, and suction artifacts. Mandatory inclusion of 4,275 verified negative frames to measure False Alarm Rate (FAR).
Polyps of Highest Value Large, protruding pedunculated polyps (>10 mm) that yield high spatial overlap scores. Subtle, flat, pale Sessile Serrated Lesions (SSLs) that human endoscopists miss at rates up to 35%. Size and morphology stratification (diminutive ≤5mm vs advanced ≥10mm) in evaluation benchmarks.
Inference Constraints Unconstrained batch GPU offline processing (often >100 ms per image). Strict real-time inline hardware appliance budget strictly <40 ms latency to avoid motion lag. Hardware-aware benchmarking on realistic edge video streams without frame-buffering delays.
Evaluation Integrity Random frame splitting of video sequences, leading to extensive temporal leakage. Independent patients, diverse endoscope manufacturers, and distinct hospital centers. Cryptographically verified splits_v2 protocol with zero cross-split video or sequence contamination.
