Res2Net-50 Hierarchical Multi-Branch Grouping

Replaces standard $3\times3$ bottleneck convolutions with hierarchical sub-feature groups ($s=4$). The input feature map is split into 4 filter subsets $\{x_1, x_2, x_3, x_4\}$. Each subset $x_i$ passes through a $3\times3$ conv $K_i()$, where $y_i = K_i(x_i + y_{i-1})$. This enables an exponential expansion of receptive fields within a single residual block, perfectly handling polyps ranging from 2mm to 40mm.

⚠️ Benchmark Integrity Caveat:
# Res2Net-50 Multi-Branch Feature Aggregation y_1 = x_1 y_2 = Conv3x3_2(x_2) y_3 = Conv3x3_3(x_3 + y_2) y_4 = Conv3x3_4(x_4 + y_3) out = Concat([y_1, y_2, y_3, y_4]) # Output shape: [B, C, H/32, W/32]

FOUNDATIONAL ARCHITECTURE • 2015

1. U-Net: Convolutional Networks for Biomedical Segmentation

Olaf Ronneberger, Philipp Fischer, Thomas Brox • MICCAI 2015, LNCS 9351, pp. 234–241
31.03M
Parameters • 53 FPS

U-Net established the dominant structural paradigm in medical imaging: an encoder-decoder network consisting of a contracting path to capture multi-scale semantic context and an expanding symmetric path that enables precise pixel-level localization.

Contracting Path (Encoder)

  • Repeated application of two $3\times3$ unpadded convolutions followed by a Rectified Linear Unit (ReLU).
  • $2\times2$ max-pooling operation with stride 2 for spatial downsampling.
  • Channel progression doubles at each stage: $64 \rightarrow 128 \rightarrow 256 \rightarrow 512 \rightarrow 1024$.
  • Bottleneck connects deep semantics at spatial resolution $H/16 \times W/16$ with 1024 feature channels.

Expanding Path & Skip Connections

  • Every expanding step applies an up-convolution ($2\times2$ transposed convolution) halving channel dimensions.
  • Identity Skip Connections: Direct channel concatenation with the cropped feature map from the corresponding contracting path stage.
  • Two consecutive $3\times3$ convolutions followed by ReLU to reassemble high-resolution spatial boundaries.
  • Final layer: $1\times1$ convolution mapping 64-channel features to 2-class background/polyp logits.

Weighted Cross-Entropy Loss with Distance Boundary Penalty

To enforce crisp separation between touching cell margins and mucosal borders, Ronneberger et al. formulated a precomputed morphological distance weight map:

$$\mathcal{L}_{UNet} = \sum_{x \in \Omega} w(x) \log\left(p_{l(x)}(x)\right)$$ $$w(x) = w_c(x) + w_0 \cdot \exp\left( -\frac{(d_1(x) + d_2(x))^2}{2\sigma^2} \right)$$

Where $w_c(x)$ balances background-to-polyp class frequencies, and $d_1(x), d_2(x)$ represent Euclidean distances to the nearest and second-nearest object boundaries.

Failure Modes in Endoscopic Practice

When evaluated on out-of-distribution multicenter datasets, U-Net collapses catastrophically: achieving only 0.398 mDice on ETIS-Larib and 0.512 on CVC-ColonDB. Plain skip connections propagate specular lighting glare and mucosal folds, producing ragged, leaky boundaries around sessile lesions.

MICCAI 2020 ORAL • SOTA BASELINE

2. PraNet: Parallel Reverse Attention Network

Deng-Ping Fan, Ge-Peng Ji, Tao Zhou, Geng Chen, Huazhu Fu, Jianbing Shen, Ling Shao • MICCAI 2020
32.55M
Parameters • 50 FPS

PraNet transformed polyp segmentation by introducing the Boundary Mining Paradigm: instead of attempting to predict boundaries directly from noisy low-level features, PraNet first locates the polyp core using a coarse global saliency map, erases the core via inverted attention, and forces subsequent decoder layers to refine the remaining peripheral tissue margins.

1. Res2Net-50 Backbone

Splits $3\times3$ filters into 4 multi-scale scale-dimension sub-groups ($s=4$). Yields an exponential expansion of receptive fields, matching diverse polyp morphologies from 2mm diminutive lesions to large adenomas.

2. PPD Module

Discards shallow features $f_1, f_2$ entirely. Fuses top semantic stages $\{f_3, f_4, f_5\}$ to generate a coarse initial saliency map $S_g$ that pinpoints the lesion body without crisp borders.

3. Reverse Attention

Stacked modules ($RA_5 \rightarrow RA_4 \rightarrow RA_3$) multiply high-level features by the inverted prediction: $A_i = 1 - \sigma(P_{i+1})$. Forces the network to ignore the interior and recover fine perimeter transitions.

PraNet Protocol A Benchmark Results:
Kvasir-SEG: 0.898 mDice • CVC-ColonDB: 0.709 mDice • ETIS-Larib: 0.628 mDice • CVC-ClinicDB: 0.899 mDice*
*Video Leakage Caveat: The 0.899 ClinicDB score evaluates on 62 test frames sharing identical video sequences with training frames (30 near-duplicate links with $\ge 20$ RANSAC inliers), causing +12% to +15% artificial Dice inflation via seen-domain memorization. See Benchmark Integrity Audit.

PraNet Structure Loss Formulation

PraNet employs a boundary-weighted combination of Weighted Binary Cross-Entropy ($\mathcal{L}_{wbce}$) and Weighted IoU ($\mathcal{L}_{wiou}$) with deep supervision across all 4 decoder heads:

$$\mathcal{L}_{total} = \mathcal{L}(S_g, G) + \sum_{i=3}^5 \mathcal{L}(S_i, G)$$ $$\mathcal{L}(P, G) = \mathcal{L}_{wbce}(P, G) + \mathcal{L}_{wiou}(P, G)$$ $$\text{weight}_{ij} = 1 + 5 \cdot \alpha_{ij}, \quad \alpha_{ij} = \left| \frac{\sum_{k,l \in A_{ij}} G_{kl}}{|A_{ij}|} - G_{ij} \right|$$

A $31\times31$ average pooling filter over ground truth mask $G$ computes pixel significance $\alpha_{ij}$. For uniform background or interior, $\alpha_{ij} \approx 0 \implies \text{weight} \approx 1.0$; near lesion margins, $\alpha_{ij} \approx 0.5 \implies \text{weight} \approx 3.5$, boosting boundary gradients by 350%.

EFFICIENCY BREAKTHROUGH • 2021

3. HarDNet-MSEG: Memory-Traffic Optimized Real-Time Segmentation

Chien-Hsiang Huang, Hung-Yu Wu, Youn-Long Lin • National Tsing Hua University, Taiwan
88 FPS
33.34M Params • 10.4 GFLOPs

Huang et al. identified that theoretical FLOPs correlate poorly with real-world GPU latency. Instead, modern GPU inference is constrained by DRAM memory bandwidth. HarDNet-MSEG minimizes Convolutional Input/Output (CIO) traffic while maintaining SOTA segmentation accuracy.

Harmonic Dense Connections (HarDNet68)

DenseNet concatenates all preceding layers, creating severe GPU memory read bottlenecks. HarDNet introduces power-of-two harmonic connections: layer $k$ connects only to layer $k + 2^n$. This keeps shortcut connections sparse, maximizes channel widths, and slashes DRAM access latency.

$$\text{CIO} = \sum_{l} \left( C_{in}^{(l)} \times H_{in} \times W_{in} + C_{out}^{(l)} \times H_{out} \times W_{out} \right)$$

Cascaded Partial Decoder & RFB

Employs a Cascaded Partial Decoder combined with Receptive Field Blocks (RFB) on skip connections. Crucially, the authors deliberately placed zero HarDBlocks in the decoder: keeping the decoder computationally lightweight prevents memory stalls and enables 86–88 FPS on standard GeForce RTX 2080 Ti GPUs.

Metrics: Kvasir-SEG: 0.904 mDice (0.857 mIoU) • ETIS-Larib: 0.677 mDice • CVC-ClinicDB: 0.932 mDice*
*Video Leakage Caveat: 0.932 ClinicDB score is an in-sequence video memorization artifact (+12–15% inflation). See Benchmark Integrity Audit.
The Resolution Mismatch Nuance

HarDNet-MSEG trains and tests at $312 \times 312$ resolution (97,344 pixels), whereas PraNet, SANet, Polyp-PVT, and ColonFormer evaluate at $352 \times 352$ (123,904 pixels). HarDNet processes 21.4% fewer pixels per frame, giving it an inherent structural latency advantage. Cross-paper latency rankings must account for input resolution normalization.

TRANSFORMER MILESTONE • 2021

4. Polyp-PVT: Pyramid Vision Transformer with Graph Reasoning

Bo Dong, Wenhai Wang, Deng-Ping Fan, Jinpeng Li, Huazhu Fu, Ling Shao • arXiv:2108.07368 / CAAI TIT
44.60M
Params • 0.808 ColonDB Dice

Polyp-PVT replaced convolutional backbones with a hierarchical Pyramid Vision Transformer (PVTv2-B2). By formulating polyp segmentation as a camouflaged object detection challenge and incorporating Graph Convolutional Networks (GCN), Polyp-PVT achieved unprecedented rotational invariance against colonoscope torque.

PVTv2-B2 Backbone

Non-overlapping $4\times4$ patch projection. Uses Linear Spatial-Reduction Attention (SRA) to cut attention complexity from $\mathcal{O}(N^2)$ to $\mathcal{O}(N^2/R)$. Mix-FFN depth-wise convs dynamically inject positional bias without fixed coordinate embeddings.

Camouflaged Detection (CIM)

Operates on shallow feature $f_2$. Applies sequential channel attention followed by large-kernel ($7\times7$) spatial attention to unmask lesions whose color and mucosal pit patterns blend seamlessly with surrounding healthy tissue.

Graph-SAM (Rotational Invariance)

Fuses high-level semantic tokens $T_h$ with boundary features $T_l$ using Graph Convolution Networks: $\mathcal{G} = \tilde{D}^{-\frac{1}{2}} \tilde{A} \tilde{D}^{-\frac{1}{2}} X W$. In an ablation with $180^\circ$ scope rotation, GCN retained 0.806 mDice vs 0.756 without GCN.

Historical Milestone: Polyp-PVT was the first architecture in published medical literature to surpass the 0.80 mDice barrier on unseen CVC-ColonDB (0.808), establishing Vision Transformers as the benchmark standard for out-of-distribution generalizability.
Kvasir-SEG: 0.917 mDice • CVC-ColonDB: 0.808 mDice • ETIS-Larib: 0.787 mDice • CVC-ClinicDB: 0.937 mDice*
*Video Leakage Caveat: While Polyp-PVT achieved genuine breakthrough generalization on unseen ColonDB (0.808), its 0.937 ClinicDB score represents seen-domain video interpolation. See Benchmark Integrity Audit.
IEEE ACCESS / JBHI 2022 • HIERARCHICAL VIT

5. ColonFormer: Residual Axial Reverse Attention

Nguyen Thanh Duc, Nguyen Thi Oanh, Nguyen Thi Thuy, Tran Minh Triet, Dinh Viet Sang • IEEE Access 2022
46.64M
MiT-B3 • 0.801 ETIS Dice

ColonFormer couples SegFormer's Mix Transformer (MiT-B3) with a UPer Decoder, Pyramid Pooling Module (PPM), Cross-level Feature Pyramid (CFP), and Residual Axial Reverse Attention (RA-RA).

Residual Axial Reverse Attention (RA-RA)

Replaces conventional reverse attention with axial attention decomposed along horizontal and vertical image axes. Crucially, ColonFormer incorporates an identity residual escape hatch: because axial attention can over-suppress valid features in irregularly shaped polyps, the network dynamically bypasses axial layers when they fail to provide constructive gradients.

Weighted Focal & IoU Loss Averaging

ColonFormer replaces Binary Cross-Entropy with Weighted Focal Loss ($\mathcal{L}_{wfocal}$) and averages rather than sums the two loss components:

$$\mathcal{L}_{ColonFormer} = \frac{1}{2} \left( \mathcal{L}_{wfocal} + \mathcal{L}_{wiou} \right)$$

This halving of effective gradient magnitude stabilized transformer fine-tuning on limited medical samples.

The Non-Monotonic Backbone Scaling Discovery

Duc et al. evaluated scaling across Mix Transformer variants (MiT-B1 to B5):
• MiT-B1 (XS): 0.913 Kvasir / 0.779 ETIS
• MiT-B2 (S): 0.927 Kvasir / 0.789 ETIS
• MiT-B3 (L): 0.924 Kvasir / 0.801 ETIS (Peak Performance)
• MiT-B4 (XL): 0.920 Kvasir / 0.776 ETIS
• MiT-B5 (XXL): 0.920 Kvasir / 0.775 ETIS (−2.6% degradation)
Core Finding: Scaling beyond MiT-B3 systematically degrades out-of-distribution generalizability. Heavy foundation backbones overfit rapidly when fine-tuned on standard 1,450-image clinical training sets.

MiT-B3 Peak Metrics: Kvasir-SEG: 0.924 mDice • ETIS-Larib: 0.801 mDice • CVC-ColonDB: 0.814 mDice • CVC-ClinicDB: 0.941 mDice*
*Video Leakage Caveat: 0.941 ClinicDB score reflects seen-domain video frame leakage across training and test sequences. See Benchmark Integrity Audit.
STATE SPACE FOUNDATION SOTA • 2024/2025

6. EndoMamba: Real-Time Selective State Space Video Foundation Model

Wang et al. • MICCAI 2024 / Springer 2025
46.9 FPS
Constant at T=128 • 25.4M Params

EndoMamba solved the critical Video Complexity Wall that crippled prior video transformers in clinical endoscopy. By replacing quadratic spatiotemporal attention with input-dependent selective state space models (Mamba S6), EndoMamba processes continuous colonoscopy video with linear complexity $\mathcal{O}(P \cdot T \cdot m)$.

The Video Complexity Wall

In video endoscopy, standard vision transformers (e.g., Endo-FM) compute space-time attention across patches $P$ and time frames $T$, scaling quadratically:

$$\text{Complexity}_{Transformer} = \mathcal{O}(P^2 T^2 m)$$

At $T=32$ frames, Endo-FM throughput falls to 9.2 FPS; at $T=128$, it plummets to 2.4 FPS, rendering it clinically unusable for live procedure assistance.

Selective State Space Mechanics (S6)

Replaces self-attention with continuous-time dynamical state equations discretized via Zero-Order Hold (ZOH):

$$h_t = \bar{A} h_{t-1} + \bar{B} x_t, \quad y_t = C h_t + D x_t$$ $$\text{Complexity}_{EndoMamba} = \mathcal{O}(P \cdot T \cdot m)$$

The recurrent hidden state $h_t$ continuously carries temporal memory. Video sequence length $T$ is computationally free during inference, sustaining flat 46.9 FPS at $T=128$ frames.

Pretraining Scale & Benchmark Performance

Pretrained on MIX12 (74,828 video clips comprising 11,303,853 frames) using cross-view matching and dynamic motion matching across variable scope withdrawal speeds.

CVC-12k Video Dice: 85.4% (vs Endo-FM 78.5%) PolypDiag F1: 95.0% (vs Endo-FM 90.7%) Throughput: 47.1 FPS (T=32) / 46.9 FPS (T=128)
