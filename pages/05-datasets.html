Dataset Integrity & Evaluation Guardrails

Scientific benchmark integrity requires strict adherence to three verified rules:
1. Never pool or merge CVC-ColonDB (380 frames) and CVC-300 (60 frames): 58 of the 60 frames in CVC-300 are exact-pixel duplicates of CVC-ColonDB.
2. Acknowledge CVC-ClinicDB Video Leakage: All 62 test frames in PraNet's Protocol A share identical video sequences with training frames, creating artificial seen-domain memorization (+12% to +15% mDice).
3. Zero New Segmented Data in HyperKvasir: All 1,000 segmented polyp images in HyperKvasir are byte-for-byte SHA-256 identical copies of Kvasir-SEG.

Detailed Dataset Profiles

Displaying 10 of 10 datasets
Barcelona, Spain • 2015 Video Leakage Hazard

CVC-ClinicDB (CVC-612)

Bernal et al. (Computerized Medical Imaging and Graphics, 2015). Captured at Hospital Clínic de Barcelona. Standard baseline for polyp localization.

  • Scale:612 frames (31 clips, 13 patients)
  • Resolution:384 × 288 (SD WLI)
  • Modality:White Light Imaging (WLI)
  • Annotations:Dense Binary Pixel Masks
  • Benchmark Split:Protocol A Train (550) / Seen Test (62)
Forensic Finding: All 62 test frames originate from the same 13-patient video sequences as training frames (30 verified near-duplicate links). Tests seen-domain memorization, not OOD generalization.
Barcelona, Spain • 2012 CVC-300 Overlap (58)

CVC-ColonDB

Bernal et al. (2012, IEEE TMI). Foundational dataset developed to evaluate Window Median Depth of Valleys Accumulation (WM-DOVA).

  • Scale:380 static frames (15 colonoscopies)
  • Resolution:574 × 500 (or 500 × 574)
  • Modality:White Light Imaging (WLI)
  • Annotations:Binary Pixel Segmentation Masks
  • Benchmark Split:Protocol A Unseen Out-of-Distribution Test
Integrity Mandate: Never pool with CVC-300 / EndoScene. 58 of the 60 frames in CVC-300 are exact-pixel duplicates of CVC-ColonDB. Contains 15 internal duplicate clusters.
Paris, France • 2014 Verified Clean OOD

ETIS-LaribPolypDB

Silva et al. (2014). Captured at Lariboisière Hospital, Paris. The gold-standard clinical challenge benchmark for out-of-distribution generalizability.

  • Scale:196 static images (34 unique polyps)
  • Resolution:1225 × 970 (High Definition)
  • Modality:HD White Light Imaging (HD-WLI)
  • Annotations:Gastroenterologist-Verified Masks
  • Benchmark Split:Protocol A Hard Unseen Test (196 images)
Clinical Nuance: Contains flat (Paris IIb), sessile, and diminutive (≤5mm) polyps under challenging lighting. Models dropping from 0.93 to 0.40 reflect real-world clinical risk.
Bærum, Norway • 2020 Standard Training Pool

Kvasir-SEG

Jha et al. (MMM 2020, SimulaMet). Captured at Bærum Hospital, Vestre Viken Health Trust using Olympus Europe colonoscopy systems.

  • Scale:1,000 polyp images (routine screening)
  • Resolution:332 × 487 to 1920 × 1072 (Multi-Res)
  • Modality:White Light Imaging (WLI)
  • Annotations:Pixel Masks + Bounding Boxes + JSON
  • Benchmark Split:Protocol A Train (548) / Test (100)
Size Stratification Bias: 700 large polyps (>160px; 70%), 323 medium (32.3%), and only 48 small polyps (<64px; 4.8%). High aggregate Dice masks failure on diminutive lesions.
Oslo, Norway • 2020 1,000 Byte-for-Byte Copies

HyperKvasir

Borgli et al. (Nature Scientific Data, 2020). Massive multi-modal GI dataset comprising 110,079 images and 374 video recordings across 23 classes.

  • Scale:110,079 images total (1,000 segmented)
  • Resolution:Variable SD to Full HD
  • Modality:WLI + Narrow Band Imaging (NBI)
  • Annotations:1k Masks, 8.2k Anatomical Labels
  • Pretraining Role:Self-supervised / anatomical classification
Cryptographic Audit: All 1,000 segmented images are 1,000/1,000 SHA-256 byte-identical duplicates of Kvasir-SEG. Provides zero additional segmented training data.
Hanoi, Vietnam • 2021 CADx Multi-Class

BKAI-IGH NeoPolyp

VinUni-VinBrain & Institute of Gastroenterology and Hepatology (IGH), Hanoi, Vietnam. First public multi-class neoplastic segmentation challenge.

  • Scale:1,200 endoscopic images
  • Resolution:1280 × 1024 (HD)
  • Modality:Paired WLI & NBI
  • Annotations:Neoplastic vs Non-Neoplastic Masks
  • Clinical Paradigm:Bridges CADe and CADx Optical Biopsy
Clinical Role: Evaluates the Resect and Discard paradigm: optical differentiation of precancerous adenomas from benign hyperplastic polyps.
Móstoles, Spain • 2021 Verified Zero Leakage

PICCOLO

Sánchez-Peralta et al. (2021). Hospital Universitario de Móstoles, Madrid. High-definition multi-modal cohort with exhaustive clinical morphological metadata.

  • Scale:3,433 frames (76 lesions, 40 videos)
  • Resolution:1920 × 1080 (Full HD)
  • Modality:Paired WLI and NBI
  • Annotations:Masks + Paris + Size + Histology
  • Size Breakdown:21 Diminutive (≤5mm), 16 Small, 31 Large
Integrity Excellence: Sequence-aware patient-level partitioning with zero split leakage. Complete histopathology and Paris classification (0-I vs 0-II).
Multi-Center China • 2021 Video Detection

LDPolypVideo

Ma et al. (2021). Large-scale multi-center video dataset documenting real-world clinical noise, water-jet debris, camera motion blur, and specular glare.

  • Scale:160+ polyp videos (~42,000 frames)
  • Negative Control:61 polyp-free negative videos
  • Modality:White Light Video Colonoscopy
  • Annotations:Per-Frame Bounding Boxes
  • Clinical Nuance:Realistic colonoscope motion blur & lens glare
Benchmark Role: Crucial for measuring real-time temporal detection stability and bounding box tracker jitter during rapid scope insertion and withdrawal.
Yokohama, Japan • 2022 69% Negative Omission

SUN-SEG (SUN Database)

Ji et al. (MICCAI 2022, Showa University Northern Yokohama Hospital). Premier video polyp segmentation (VPS) benchmark with 10 fine-grained clinical attributes.

  • Total Scale:158,690 frames (1,013 sequences)
  • Positive Frames:49,136 frames (378 positive clips)
  • Negative Frames:109,554 frames (728 polyp-free clips)
  • Resolution:1280 × 720 (HD)
  • Attribute Tags:Small Objects, Motion Blur, Specular Glare
Structural Omission: 69% of SUN-SEG (109,554 frames) consists of healthy mucosa. Standard VPS papers discard all negative clips, evaluating only on positive frames and concealing false alarm rates.
6 European Centers • 2021/2023 Multi-Center Gold Standard

PolypGen 2021

Ali et al. (Nature Scientific Data, 2023). Multi-Center Consortium across 6 European hospitals (UK, France, Italy, Norway, Egypt) capturing multi-vendor diversity.

  • Scale:>10,000 curated frames
  • Sequence Positives:2,409 video frames
  • Single Positives:3,762 still images
  • Sequence Negatives:4,275 polyp-free mucosa frames
  • Hardware Vendors:Olympus, Fujifilm, Pentax HD systems
Clinical Benchmark Role: The most rigorous public benchmark for true multi-center domain generalization. Evaluates cross-vendor sensor noise, white balance shifts, and false alarms on negative mucosa.
