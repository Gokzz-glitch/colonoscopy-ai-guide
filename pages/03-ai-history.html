Core Historical Paradigm Shift

Colonoscopy computer vision evolved through four distinct architectural transformations. While early methods relied on handcrafted heuristics that failed on flat lesions, modern architectures leverage inductive biases, boundary-weighted loss functions, and long-range spatiotemporal memory to achieve real-time inference without violating the mandatory ≤40 ms clinical latency ceiling.

ERA 1 • 1990s – 2014 PARADIGM: Handcrafted Morphology & Classical ML

The Heuristic Frontier: Texture Wavelets, Energy Valleys & Geometric Voting

Prior to the deep learning revolution, researchers modeled polyps as protruding, bulbous elliptical structures using color texture wavelets, Window Median Depth of Valleys Accumulation (WM-DOVA), and radial edge voting. These systems required labor-intensive feature engineering, lacked boundary segmentation, and generated prohibitive false-positive rates on normal mucosal folds.

Key Milestones: Karkanis (2003), Bernal WM-DOVA (2012/2015), Tajbakhsh Edge-Voting (2015)
Avg. Throughput: <15 FPS (CPU / early GPU)
Fatal Limitation: Total failure on flat (Paris IIa/IIb) and sessile serrated lesions

1990s – 2014 • Era 1

WM-DOVA Energy Maps

Bernal et al. (2012, IEEE TMI) • Hospital Clínic de Barcelona
Classical CV

Introduced Window Median Depth of Valleys Accumulation (WM-DOVA), projecting directional intensity valleys to detect the protruding elliptical geometry of polyps against the tubular colon lumen.

Primary Breakthrough: First mathematical model allowing automated localization without prior contrast dyes; directly created the benchmark datasets CVC-ColonDB and CVC-ClinicDB.
Key Limitation: Incapable of producing pixel segmentation masks; false-positive rate exceeded 20 per procedure on normal colonic haustral folds.
Params: 0 (Handcrafted) Speed: ~4 FPS (CPU) Output: Energy Peak (x, y)
2015 • Era 1 Transition

Geometric Edge-Voting CADe

Tajbakhsh, Gurudu, & Liang (2015, IEEE TMI) • Mayo Clinic
Geometric ML

Coupled edge classification with global geometric constraints and radial edge-voting, demonstrating that boundary edge geometry vastly outperforms raw color histograms in distinguishing mucosal tissue from vascular patterns.

Primary Breakthrough: Proved that learned edge orientation filters dramatically outperform color-texture descriptors across multi-patient cohorts.
Key Limitation: Severe performance collapse on flat, diminutive (≤5mm) lesions lacking elevated geometric boundaries (Paris IIa/IIb).
Params: N/A (Random Forest/SVM) Speed: 10 FPS Resolution: 512 × 512
2015 • Era 2

U-Net Biomedical ConvNet

Ronneberger et al. (2015, MICCAI) • University of Freiburg
Deep CNN

The foundational medical segmentation architecture. Introduced symmetric contracting (encoder) and expanding (decoder) paths bridged by identity skip connections, enabling precise pixel-level localization with modest training data.

Primary Breakthrough: Complete end-to-end dense pixel segmentation; identity skip connections preserved high-frequency spatial details.
Key Limitation: Massive out-of-distribution collapse: plummets to 0.398 mDice on ETIS-Larib and 0.512 on ColonDB. Ragged boundaries on low-contrast mucosa.
Params: 31.03M Speed: 53 FPS (FP32) Kvasir Dice: 0.818
2018 • Era 2 Clinical

SegNet Real-Time CADe

Wang et al. (2018, Lancet Gastro Hep) • Sichuan University
First Live RCT

Trained on 27,113 frames from 1,138 patients, this VGG-16 based SegNet system powered the world's first prospective randomized controlled trial, proving AI directly boosts Adenoma Detection Rate (ADR).

Primary Breakthrough: Verified 94.4% sensitivity and 95.9% specificity at 25 FPS with real-time inline 77 ms latency.
Key Limitation: High false-alarm rate (FAR) on fluid bubbles and mucosal reflections, causing clinical alarm fatigue.
Params: 29.4M Speed: 25 FPS Latency: 77 ms
2020 • Era 2 Oral

PraNet: Parallel Reverse Attention

Fan et al. (2020, MICCAI Oral) • IIAI / Inception Institute
Boundary Mining

Coupled a Res2Net-50 multi-scale backbone with a Parallel Partial Decoder (PPD) and a top-down Reverse Attention (RA) cascade, establishing the standard modern polyp segmentation architecture.

Primary Breakthrough: Erased the detected polyp interior via inverted attention (1 - σ(P)) to force the network to recursively refine ambiguous boundary transitions.
Key Limitation: Discarded shallow features ($f_1, f_2$) to save compute, causing performance drops on subtle diminutive polyps (<5mm).
Params: 32.55M Speed: 50 FPS ETIS Dice: 0.628
2021 • Era 2 Efficiency

HarDNet-MSEG: Low-CIO Real-Time

Huang et al. (2021, arXiv:2101.07172) • NTHU Taiwan
88 FPS SOTA

Optimized for GPU memory access cost (Convolutional Input/Output - CIO) rather than theoretical FLOPs, using harmonic power-of-two dense connections to achieve ultra-fast real-time inference.

Primary Breakthrough: Delivered 88 FPS on standard GeForce RTX 2080 Ti hardware while matching or exceeding PraNet accuracy (0.904 Kvasir mDice).
Key Limitation: Resolution mismatch: trained and tested at $312 \times 312$ (21.4% fewer pixels than $352 \times 352$), partially driving its speed advantage.
Params: 33.34M Speed: 88 FPS (RTX 2080 Ti) GFLOPs: 10.4 G
2021 • Era 2 MICCAI

SANet: Shallow Attention & Color Exchange

Wei et al. (2021, MICCAI) • Institute of Automation, CAS
Color Invariance

Challenged the convention of discarding low-level features by introducing the Shallow Attention Module (SAM) and CIELAB Color Exchange (CE) data augmentation to bridge multi-center lighting variations.

Primary Breakthrough: Massive jump on unseen ETIS-Larib from 0.628 (PraNet) to 0.750 mDice, driven by preserving shallow boundary features for diminutive polyps.
Key Limitation: CIELAB color normalization can alter subtle mucosal vascular patterns crucial for neoplastic differentiation (NICE criteria).
Params: 32.55M Speed: 72 FPS ETIS Dice: 0.750
2021 • Era 3 Transformer

Polyp-PVT: Pyramid Vision Transformer

Dong et al. (2021, arXiv:2108.07368 / CAAI TIT)
PVTv2 + GCN

Pioneered pure Vision Transformer backbones for endoscopy. Combined PVTv2-B2 with a Cascaded Fusion Module (CFM), Camouflaged Identification Module (CIM), and Graph Convolutional Similarity Aggregation.

Primary Breakthrough: First architecture in medical literature to cross the 0.80 mDice threshold on unseen CVC-ColonDB (0.808); GCN provided scope rotational invariance.
Key Limitation: Higher parameter footprint (44.6M) and slower inference (30 FPS), operating close to the clinical real-time limit.
Params: 44.60M Speed: 30 FPS ColonDB Dice: 0.808
2022 • Era 3 MICCAI

SSFormer: Local Guides Global

Wang et al. (2022, MICCAI) • Shanghai Jiao Tong University
Locality Decoder

Diagnosed that unconstrained ViT self-attention diffuses probability mass globally. Introduced Local Emphasis (LE) modules in a Progressive Locality Decoder (PLD) to re-concentrate attention onto local lesion borders.

Primary Breakthrough: Re-injected local inductive bias into transformer attention, achieving 0.796 mDice on ETIS-Larib with 33.5M parameters.
Key Limitation: Throughput drops to 28 FPS on Tesla P100 due to multi-scale feature interpolation bottlenecks.
Params: 33.50M Speed: 28 FPS ETIS Dice: 0.796
2022 • Era 3 IEEE

ColonFormer: Residual Axial Reverse Attention

Duc et al. (2022, IEEE Access / JBHI) • HUST / VNU
SegFormer MiT-B3

Coupled SegFormer's Mix Transformer (MiT-B3) with a UPer Decoder, Cross-level Feature Pyramid (CFP), and Residual Axial Reverse Attention (RA-RA) with an identity bypass escape hatch.

Primary Breakthrough: Pushed unseen ETIS-Larib mDice to 0.801; discovered the critical Non-Monotonic Backbone Scaling Law in medical vision.
Key Limitation: Heavy backbone scaling (MiT-B4/B5) overfits on medical splits, dropping ETIS Dice by 2.6 percentage points (to 0.775).
Params: 46.64M Speed: 22 FPS (RTX 2080 Ti) ColonDB Dice: 0.814
2022 • Era 3 Benchmark Peak

HSNet: Hybrid Semantic Network

Fu et al. (2022, Comp Biol Med)
0.808 ETIS Peak

Combined filtered shallow boundary details with deep semantic representations to bridge the semantic gap, setting the current unprompted benchmark ceiling across standard static datasets.

Primary Breakthrough: Holds the highest published static benchmark scores: 0.926 Kvasir, 0.937 ClinicDB*, and 0.808 ETIS-Larib mDice.
*Integrity Disclaimer: CVC-ClinicDB scores suffer from in-sequence video frame temporal leakage (+12% to +15% artificial Dice inflation from seen-domain interpolation); see Benchmark Integrity Audit.
Key Limitation: 22 FPS throughput on RTX 3090 fails 60 FPS ultra-HD clinical colonoscope integration requirements.
Params: 46.64M Speed: 22 FPS ETIS Dice: 0.808
2023 • Era 4 Foundation

Endo-FM: Video Foundation Model

Wang et al. (2023, MICCAI) • CUHK / Shanghai AI Lab
Space-Time ViT-B

Pioneered large-scale self-supervised pretraining for endoscopic video. Trained a 121.3M parameter ViT-B/16 with divided space-time attention across 5 million frames (33,000 endoscopic clips).

Primary Breakthrough: Massive self-supervised pretraining established foundation model representations tailored to gastrointestinal endoscopy.
Key Limitation: Quadratic attention complexity $\mathcal{O}(P^2 T^2 m)$ caused severe throughput collapse: drops to 9.2 FPS at T=32 and 2.4 FPS at T=128 frames.
Params: 121.3M Speed: 2.4 FPS (T=128) Complexity: Quadratic O(P²T²m)
2024/2025 • Era 4 SOTA

EndoMamba: Selective State Space

Wang et al. (2024, MICCAI / Springer 2025)
Mamba S6

Replaced transformer attention with selective state space models (Mamba S6), executing bidirectional visual selective scans with linear complexity $\mathcal{O}(P \cdot T \cdot m)$ for continuous video colonoscopy.

Primary Breakthrough: Solved the video complexity wall: maintains flat 47.1 FPS throughput at T=32 and 46.9 FPS at T=128 frames; 85.4% Dice on CVC-12k.
Key Limitation: Requires specialized CUDA hardware kernels for hardware-aware associative scan execution.
Params: 25.40M Speed: 46.9 FPS (T=128) Complexity: Linear O(PTm)
2026 • Era 4 Distillation

LiteBounD: Foundation Distillation

Recent 2026 Frontier • Boundary Distillation Paradigm
Edge Distillation

Distills heavy foundation models (SAM, DINOv2, OneFormer) into lightweight student backbones (U-Net, PraNet) by decomposing teacher representations into high/low frequency boundary tokens.

Primary Breakthrough: Elevates standard U-Net from 0.818 to 0.869 mDice on Kvasir-SEG with only +0.3M added parameters, running at >90 FPS.
Key Limitation: Multi-stage teacher distillation requires expensive multi-teacher offline precomputation before student training.
Params: 17.0M Speed: 92 FPS Kvasir Dice: 0.869
